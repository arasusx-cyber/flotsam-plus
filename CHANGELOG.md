# Changelog

- Reveal the entire currently generated map, with optional automatic discovery of later world tiles.
- Reveal native map fog and existing locations without forcing future biomes or scouting quests.
- Independent resident walking/swimming speed multiplier from 1 to 5, including island navigation.
- Resident picker and grants of 1–100 unspent skill points per click; native spending and skill caps remain.

- Five Polish F8 menu tabs, per-feature compatibility handling, and reset support for new options.
